## Website traffic, product behavior, and telemetry

| Your application | What you can measure |
| --- | --- |
| [Website](./docs/guides/web-reports/web-reports.mdx) | Visitors, popular pages, sources, campaigns, and engagement |
| [Product](./docs/guides/product-analytics/product-analytics.mdx) | Custom actions, purchases, revenue, and conversion paths |

Use the website reports as a starting point, then build custom queries as your questions grow.
